The Platform

A digital channel from submission to outcome.

E-REVIRESCO coordinates collection, registration, assessment, partner allocation and documentation while keeping the item's journey traceable.

01

Submit

Anyone can submit unwanted electronics through the platform, including households, businesses and institutions.

02

Register

The item receives a unique identity and its source, condition, device information and supporting records are captured.

03

Assess

Technical condition, data-bearing status and possible recovery pathways are evaluated.

04

Connect

The system identifies an appropriate repair, refurbishment, recovery, recycling or specialist partner.

05

Track

Important handovers and processing events are recorded against the item's digital identity.

06

Report

The final outcome can be documented for the owner, institution, partner and E-REVIRESCO records.

Data Security

Data security is part of the recovery process.

Data-bearing equipment must be treated differently from ordinary electronic waste. The platform records the security status and the action taken.

Identify

Identify HDDs, SSDs, phones, tablets, memory cards, USB storage and other components that may contain data.

Secure Custody

Data-bearing equipment can be recorded and held securely while the appropriate security procedure is determined.

Sanitise

Use an appropriate verified sanitisation or destruction method depending on the storage medium, condition and required level of assurance.

Verify & Document

Record the method, operator, date, result, verification and final destination. Failed procedures require further action.

Repair & Technical Services

Not everything belongs in recycling.

Where an item has recoverable value, E-REVIRESCO can coordinate repair or refurbishment directly within its competence or through vetted technical partners.

SERVICE

Repair

Devices may be repaired for owners at a fee where the service is technically and economically appropriate.

PARTNER

Specialist Repair

Complex repairs can be referred to suitable technical partners through a documented partner relationship.

RECOVERY

Component Recovery

Useful components can be recovered where appropriate rather than treating every device as material for immediate recycling.

About E-REVIRESCO

Technology that gives discarded technology a documented pathway.

PROJECT E-REVIRESCO focuses on the coordination problem: helping people and organisations submit unwanted electronics, organising responsible collection and connecting each item with an appropriate downstream partner.

Mission

To build an accessible, technology-enabled system for responsible e-waste collection, coordination, traceability and recovery, while connecting communities, institutions, technical businesses and downstream partners.

Vision

A connected recovery ecosystem in which unwanted electronics can be submitted through a trusted digital channel and their journey from collection to final outcome can be responsibly documented.

Launch Focus

Western Uganda, beginning with an operational pilot and a proposed initial collection centre at NEXUS TECH HUB.

Institutional Partnership

Kabale University is considered as a proposed institutional partner for supervision, recognition, collaboration and access to relevant funding or innovation opportunities.
